i've always loved art — drawing, making things, noticing how things are put together — and somewhere along the way i realized ux design was basically that same instinct pointed at real problems.

i'm currently a senior studying urban tech at michigan, which has given me a strange but useful foundation: i think about systems, people, and space before i ever touch a screen. lately i've been leaning harder into design and visual work, with grad school in mind.

right now i'm working on a spatial redesign of the turner senior resource center, a project that's taught me a lot about designing for people whose needs are easy to overlook.
